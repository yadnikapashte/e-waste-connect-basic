About E-Waste Connect

E-Waste Connect is a platform designed to bridge the gap between households generating electronic waste and the formal recycling sector. Our mission is to promote responsible e-waste management by making it easy for individuals to schedule pickups and connect with certified recyclers.

We aim to reduce environmental pollution caused by improper disposal of electronics and support a circular economy through sustainable recycling practices.
